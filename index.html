About

The short version: I learn hard systems fast, and then I teach them to whoever comes next. That pattern has run through everything so far. Music school, then six years as a Navy Mandarin cryptologic linguist (top of my class at DLI), then neuroscience and computer science at CU Boulder, then seven years as a consulting engineer shipping healthcare and fintech systems. The newest hard system is AI tooling, and honestly it's the most fun one yet. I built the AI enablement layer for a HIPAA-regulated delivery team, wrote the guardrails, and got certified as an Anthropic architect along the way.

Off the clock I run a D&D campaign, build games and curricula for my wife and daughter, play guitar, and once built a working set of instruments out of PVC pipe. The ship you walked around up there is my pixel art. I guess the theme is: I like making things that teach people things.
